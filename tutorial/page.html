Gesh control animations

Your recorded hands on the headset's own hand mesh, replayed. Drag a view to orbit, scroll to zoom.

Recorded at 120 fps on the Quest, kept at 60 fps here. The hand is drawn the way hand.hlsl draws it: a depth pass, a dark fill pushed out 1.5 mm, and a light outline shell 2 mm past that, fading out toward the wrist. The highlight follows the app's rules: the nearest corner within 5 cm, else an edge or face within 2.5 cm, aimed from the index tip, or the middle tip while it's closing on the thumb.
